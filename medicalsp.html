Medical Specialists

At Rafiqa Medical Center, our team of highly qualified medical specialists is committed to providing expert care across a wide range of health conditions. Whether you're seeking diagnosis, treatment, or long-term management of chronic diseases, our specialists are here to guide you every step of the way.

What is a Medical Specialist?

A medical specialist is a doctor who has completed advanced education and clinical training in a specific area of medicine. Unlike general practitioners, specialists focus on particular diseases, body systems, or types of treatment.

Our Specialties Include:

  • Internal Medicine (Diabetes, Hypertension, Thyroid)
  • Cardiology (Heart-related conditions)
  • Gastroenterology (Digestive system issues)
  • Pulmonology (Lung and respiratory care)
  • Nephrology (Kidney-related concerns)
  • Endocrinology (Hormonal imbalances)
  • Rheumatology (Arthritis and autoimmune diseases)

Why Choose Our Specialists?

  • Board-certified and experienced doctors
  • Personalized treatment plans
  • Modern diagnostic tools
  • Compassionate care and follow-up
