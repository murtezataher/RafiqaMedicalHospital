Diagnostic Laboratory

The Rafiqa Medical Center Laboratory offers precise and timely diagnostic testing services, helping doctors make informed decisions for better patient care. We use modern equipment and strict quality control to ensure accuracy.

Our Lab Services Include:

  • Complete Blood Count (CBC)
  • Blood Glucose & Cholesterol Tests
  • Liver & Kidney Function Tests
  • Urine & Stool Analysis
  • Pregnancy & Hormone Tests
  • Infection Screening (HCV, HBV, etc.)

Why Choose Our Laboratory?

  • Automated and calibrated lab machines
  • Qualified laboratory technologists
  • Strict quality assurance & hygiene standards
  • Fast and reliable test reporting

Need a test? Visit our lab today or consult your physician for recommended diagnostics.
