About Us

Established in 1992, Rafiqa Medical Center has been a pillar of quality healthcare in the region for over three decades. The center is a trusted and recognized private medical institution dedicated to delivering exceptional care to patients of all ages. Our goal is to provide timely, affordable, and specialized medical services in a compassionate and welcoming environment.
