About Us

Established in 1992, Rafiqa Medical Center has served the Sargodha community with compassion and excellence for over three decades. Located in Satellite Town Block A, our hospital is equipped with modern facilities and a dedicated team of healthcare professionals. We strive to deliver quality medical care around the clock — 24 hours a day, 7 days a week.

Whether you're visiting us for a check-up or urgent care, we promise to provide safe, personalized treatment that meets your needs and exceeds expectations.

We are proud to offer a wide range of medical services under one roof, with a team of skilled and compassionate professionals. Our specialties include:

  • Medical Specialists for general and chronic care
  • Child Specialists (Pediatrics) for newborns, infants, and children
  • Nursery and Neonatology units equipped for newborn care
  • Laparoscopic and General Surgery, offering minimally invasive procedures
  • Gynecology & Obstetrics for women's health, maternity, and childbirth
  • Fully stocked Pharmacy for on-site prescriptions
  • In-house Laboratory for reliable and quick diagnostics
