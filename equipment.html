Advanced Medical Equipment

At Rafiqa Medical Center, we utilize state-of-the-art medical technology to ensure precise diagnosis and top-tier patient care.

Aplio 500 Platinum Series

The Aplio 500 Platinum Series is a premium ultrasound imaging system known for its exceptional image clarity, advanced Doppler capabilities, and real-time tissue visualization.

It is designed to deliver fast and precise diagnostic results, improving both patient outcomes and clinical confidence.

With its Smart Fusion and Elastography technologies, the Aplio 500 provides detailed insights into tissue stiffness and blood flow—helping doctors diagnose conditions earlier and more accurately, while ensuring a safe, comfortable, and non-invasive experience for patients.

Stryker L9000 LED Light Source

The Stryker L9000 LED Light Source is a next-generation illumination system used during endoscopic and surgical procedures.

It provides brilliant, consistent lighting while generating less heat than traditional xenon light sources—ensuring a safer, cooler, and more efficient environment for both patients and surgeons.

Its superior brightness and durability make it an essential tool for precision in minimally invasive surgeries and diagnostics.
