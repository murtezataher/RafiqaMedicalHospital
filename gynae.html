Gynaecology & Obstetrics

At Rafiqa Medical Center, our Gynaecology and Obstetrics department is dedicated to women's health at every stage of life — from adolescence to menopause and beyond. We provide compassionate, personalized care for a range of reproductive and pregnancy-related needs.

What Do Gynaecologists and Obstetricians Do?

Gynaecologists specialize in the female reproductive system, while obstetricians focus on pregnancy, childbirth, and postpartum care. Many of our consultants are trained in both fields to offer complete women’s health services.

Our Gynae & Obstetric Services Include:

  • Antenatal and postnatal care
  • Normal and C-section deliveries
  • Infertility evaluations and support
  • Ultrasound and fetal monitoring
  • Menstrual and hormonal disorder treatment
  • PCOS, fibroids, cysts, and endometriosis care

Why Choose Our Women’s Health Team?

  • Experienced female consultants
  • Privacy-focused and comfortable environment
  • Emergency obstetric care available 24/7
  • Education and counseling for mothers

Your comfort, dignity, and health are our top priorities. Visit us for trusted women’s healthcare.
