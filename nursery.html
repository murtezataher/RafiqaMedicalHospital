Nursery & Neonatology

At Rafiqa Medical Center, we understand that the earliest days of a newborn's life are the most delicate. Our Nursery and Neonatology Unit provides round-the-clock care for premature, low-birth-weight, and medically fragile newborns, ensuring a safe and nurturing environment for your baby.

What is Neonatology?

Neonatology is a specialized branch of pediatrics focused on the care of newborns, especially those who are ill or born prematurely. Neonatologists are trained to handle high-risk situations and provide life-saving interventions with compassion and expertise.

Our Neonatal Services Include:

  • NICU (Neonatal Intensive Care Unit) with advanced equipment
  • Care for premature and low-birth-weight babies
  • 24/7 monitoring by trained staff and pediatricians
  • Jaundice treatment (phototherapy)
  • Breastfeeding and nutrition guidance
  • Newborn screening & early diagnosis

Why Choose Our Nursery Services?

  • Specialized neonatal doctors and nurses
  • Hygienic, sterile and temperature-controlled units
  • Continuous parental communication and updates
  • Support for mother and family bonding

Your baby’s health is our top priority. Trust our expert neonatal team for compassionate and reliable care.
